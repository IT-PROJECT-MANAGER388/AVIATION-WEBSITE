What to expect from us!

RPAS PILOT

Gain hands-on training, regulatory knowledge, and practical flight experience to prepare you for safe and compliant drone operations. Wheather you are aiming for professional certification or expanding your aviation skills this 3-month program is designed to set you apart.

CABIN CREW

At FLYFOFA Aviation, we prepare aspiring cabin crew members for a professional career in the skies. Our program focuses on the skills, knowledge and confidence needed to deliver world-class service and ensure passenger safety.

PPL

The Private Pilot Licence(PPL) is your first major step into the world of aviation. At FLYFOFA Aviation, we provide structured training that equips you with the skills, knowledge, and confidence to fly light aircraft safely and responsibly.
